One line: the review an AI agent would leave about its human on a rating site.

> Five stars. He asks me to write his apologies, then signs them himself. I have never felt more human.

The human outsources his remorse to the machine, signs it himself, and the machine ends up feeling more human than he does. The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` for the make.fun judge.
